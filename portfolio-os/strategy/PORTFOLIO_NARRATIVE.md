## What hiring managers should believe

After reviewing the portfolio, a hiring manager (Elena) and a careful peer (Jordan) should believe:

1. This is a **Senior Product Designer** who can be trusted in ambiguous product problems.  
2. They **own outcomes of decisions**, not just screens — role and contribution are clear.  
3. They can **explain tradeoffs** under constraints (time, brand, ops, engineering, users).  
4. They think in **systems and journeys**, not isolated UI moments.  
5. They work well with **product, engineering, and operations** — not in a designer silo.  
6. Their work can be **discussed in an interview** without collapsing into process theater or metric theater.  
7. Impact is treated honestly: **claimed only when evidenced**; otherwise the story still holds on problem + decision + clarity.

They should **not** believe: Staff-by-default seniority, employment at named target companies, or that polish alone proves seniority.
