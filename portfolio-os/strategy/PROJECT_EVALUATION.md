## Scoring scale

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Little or no evidence for this criterion |
| 2 | Weak / thin evidence |
| 3 | Adequate evidence or clear potential with gaps |
| 4 | Strong evidence aligned to Senior PD hire bar |
| 5 | Excellent evidence; hard to dismiss in interview |

If evidence is missing, score stays low/mid and the gap is listed under Missing evidence — **do not invent**.

## Weighting

Visual quality must not dominate. Heavier weight on hire-critical criteria:

| # | Criterion | Weight | Rationale |
|---|-----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Hiring relevance | **2.0** | Must serve Senior PD + target company proof needs |
| 2 | Problem complexity | 1.0 | Ambiguity signal |
| 3 | Decision quality | **2.0** | Core of decision-making portfolio |
| 4 | Ownership clarity | **2.0** | Jordan/Elena trust gate |
| 5 | Business or user impact | 1.0 | Valuable only when credible |
| 6 | Research depth | 1.0 | Supports story; not a substitute for decisions |
| 7 | Systems thinking | **1.5** | Required for platform / B2B / big-tech clusters |
| 8 | Cross-functional collaboration | 1.0 | Panel realism (Dev/Sam) |
| 9 | Leadership signal | 1.0 | Without exaggeration |
| 10 | Story potential | **2.0** | Case study viability |
| 11 | Visual evidence | 1.0 | Needed artefacts — not polish score |
| 12 | Public safety | 1.0 | Publish gate (floor, not differentiator) |
| 13 | Differentiation | 1.0 | Slate diversity |
| 14 | Recency | 1.0 | Current capability signal |
| 15 | Interview conversion potential | **2.0** | Elena “yes, interview” |

**Weight sum** = 20.5  

**Weighted score** = Σ(criterion score × weight) / 20.5  
Reported on the same 1–5 scale.
