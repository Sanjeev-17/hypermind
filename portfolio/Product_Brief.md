## 1. Portfolio objective

Ship a **decision-making portfolio** whose job is **interview conversion** for Senior Product Designer roles.

It is a hiring product, not a visual gallery and not a peer-design award submission.

**Confirmed.**

---

## 2. Primary hiring outcome

After a short visit, a recruiter or hiring manager can say **yes, interview** — and take a next step (save, forward, or contact) without hunting.

**Needs validation:** Instrument how “yes” is measured (reply, screen, onsite).

## 16. Time-boxed impressions

### 30 seconds

One read: **who** (Senior Product Designer) · **stake** (complex problems, clear thinking) · **category** (decision-making work) · **one proof teaser** · **one CTA**.

If the brand line were removed, it should still not feel like a generic template gallery — the *decision* promise must carry.

### 5 minutes

Can cite a problem solved, one real decision/tradeoff, and ownership. Recruiter can forward; HM can choose interview.

### 20 minutes

One flagship case holds under scrutiny: context → insight → options → decision → honest outcome → reflection.

**Needs validation:** Flagship case ID (Case_Slate).
