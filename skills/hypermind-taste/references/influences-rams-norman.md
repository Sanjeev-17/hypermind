# Influences — Rams & Norman

Sanjeev cites **Dieter Rams** and **Don Norman** as enduring inspirations. They are not visual moodboards; they are judgment systems.

| Influence | Skill | Job |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Dieter Rams | `skills/dieter-rams/SKILL.md` | Worthiness, honesty, restraint, long-lasting essentials (“less but better”) |
| Don Norman | `skills/don-norman/SKILL.md` | Discoverability, signifiers, feedback, mapping, errors, conceptual models |

## How they fit Hypermind Taste

```text
Taste (dialect / surface)
  + Rams (should this exist / is it honest / is it too much?)
  + Norman (can a person act and understand the result?)
  = shippable design
```

- Cultural / Framer surface choice → `hypermind-taste`
- Remove, simplify, honesty of claims → load **dieter-rams**
- Confusing controls, flows, empty states, “intuitive” claims → load **don-norman**

Do not flatten Rams into “gray minimal Apple UI.” Do not flatten Norman into “add more tooltips.”
